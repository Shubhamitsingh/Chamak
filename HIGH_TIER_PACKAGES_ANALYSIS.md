## 🧠 Human Psychology Analysis

### **1. The "Decoy Effect" (Anchoring)**

**What it is:**
- High-priced options make mid-tier options look more attractive
- Users compare ₹2,999 to ₹4,999 and think "₹2,999 is reasonable"
- Creates psychological anchor point

**Example:**
- Without ₹9,999: ₹4,999 seems expensive
- With ₹9,999: ₹4,999 seems like "good value"
- **Result:** More ₹2,999-₹4,999 purchases

**Verdict:** ✅ **KEEP** - They serve as psychological anchors

---

### **2. The "Aspirational Purchase" Effect**

**What it is:**
- High-tier packages create "premium" feeling
- Users aspire to buy them (even if they don't)
- Creates brand perception of "luxury/premium"

**Reality Check:**
- **Question:** Do users actually buy ₹9,999 packages?
- **Likely Answer:** Very few (< 1-2% of purchases)
- **But:** They create "premium" brand image

**Verdict:** ⚠️ **KEEP ONE** - Keep highest (₹9,999) for aspiration, remove others

---

### **3. The "Choice Overload" Problem**

**What it is:**
- Too many options = decision paralysis
- Users can't decide → don't buy anything
- 12 packages might be too many

**Current Situation:**
- 12 packages total (with new ₹9, ₹49, ₹199)
- High-tier: ₹4,999, ₹7,999, ₹9,999 (3 options)
- **Risk:** Users might get overwhelmed

**Verdict:** ⚠️ **CONSOLIDATE** - Reduce to 1-2 high-tier options

---

### **4. The "Price Sensitivity" Curve**

**Research Shows:**
- **₹0-₹500:** High purchase rate (impulse buys)
- **₹500-₹2,000:** Moderate purchase rate (considered purchases)
- **₹2,000-₹5,000:** Low purchase rate (rare purchases)
- **₹5,000+:** Very low purchase rate (< 1% of users)

**Your Packages:**
- ₹4,999 → Borderline (might get some purchases)
- ₹7,999 → Very rare purchases
- ₹9,999 → Extremely rare purchases

**Verdict:** ⚠️ **REMOVE ₹7,999** - Too high, very few buyers

---

### **5. The "Value Perception" Analysis**

**Current Value Progression:**

| Package | Coins/₹ | Value Increase |
|---------|---------|----------------|
| ₹2,999 | 15.01 | Base |
| ₹4,999 | 16.00 | +6.6% |
| ₹7,999 | 16.88 | +5.5% |
| ₹9,999 | 17.50 | +3.7% |

**Problem:**
- Diminishing returns - each step gives less value increase
- ₹7,999 → ₹9,999 only gives +3.7% more value
- Not compelling enough to justify ₹2,000 more

**Verdict:** ⚠️ **CONSOLIDATE** - Gap between ₹4,999 and ₹9,999 is too small
