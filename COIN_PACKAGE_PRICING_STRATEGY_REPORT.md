## 🧠 Human Psychology & Behavioral Insights

### **1. Price Anchoring**
- **₹9 package** creates "lowest price" anchor
- Users compare all packages to ₹9
- Makes ₹49 and ₹99 seem like great deals

### **2. Decoy Effect**
- **₹199 package** makes ₹299 look more attractive
- Users see ₹199 as "almost ₹299" but ₹299 gives much more
- Encourages upgrade to ₹299

### **3. Loss Aversion**
- **₹9 package** - "What's ₹9? I can afford that!"
- Low risk = easier decision
- Once they buy, they're "invested" in the app

### **4. Progressive Value**
- Each package shows better value than previous
- Creates "fear of missing out" (FOMO)
- Users think "I should buy bigger package for better value"

### **5. Price Points Psychology**
- **₹9** - "Less than ₹10" - feels very cheap
- **₹49** - "Less than ₹50" - psychological barrier
- **₹199** - "Less than ₹200" - mid-tier feel

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

### **1. Package Positioning**

**Entry Tier (₹9, ₹49):**
- **Purpose:** Attract new users, create first purchase
- **Strategy:** Lower value ratio, no bonus (or small bonus)
- **Marketing:** "Try it for just ₹9!" / "Perfect starter pack"

**Mid Tier (₹99, ₹199, ₹299):**
- **Purpose:** Regular users, frequent purchases
- **Strategy:** Balanced value, moderate bonuses
- **Marketing:** "Best value for regular users"

**Premium Tier (₹599+):**
- **Purpose:** Power users, heavy spenders
- **Strategy:** Best value ratio, highest bonuses
- **Marketing:** "Maximum value for serious users"

### **2. Bonus Strategy**

**Current Issue:** ₹99 has 10% bonus, ₹299 has 17% bonus - inconsistent

**Recommendation:**
- **₹9:** 0% bonus (entry level)
- **₹49:** 10% bonus (matches ₹99)
- **₹99:** 10% bonus (keep as is)
- **₹199:** 18% bonus (between ₹99 and ₹299)
- **₹299:** 20% bonus (increase from 17% to show better value)
- Continue progressive increase for higher packages

### **3. Badge Strategy**

**Current:** Only some packages have badges

**Recommendation:**
- **₹9:** No badge (entry level)
- **₹49:** "Starter" badge (encourages first purchase)
- **₹99:** "Popular Choice" (keep as is)
- **₹199:** "Smart Buy" badge (highlights value)
- Continue badges for higher packages

### **4. Visual Hierarchy**

**Recommendation:**
- **Highlight ₹9, ₹49, ₹199** with subtle border/glow
- **Make ₹99, ₹299, ₹599** stand out (most popular)
- **Premium packages** (₹999+) with special styling

## 🎯 Expected User Behavior

### **Scenario 1: New User**
1. Sees ₹9 package → "That's cheap, let me try"
2. Buys ₹9 → Uses coins quickly

4. **Result:** Regular purchases at ₹49-₹99 range
