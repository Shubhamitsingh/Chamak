## Complete Refund Policy Text

### **1. Introduction & Scope**

This Refund Policy ("Policy") governs refund requests for purchases made through the Chamakz mobile application ("App"). By making a purchase, you agree to this Refund Policy. This Policy applies to:

• Virtual currency ("Coins") purchases made through the App
• In-app purchases and digital goods
• All payment methods including UPI, Google Play Billing, and other payment gateways

This Policy is in addition to our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy. Please read this Policy carefully before making any purchase.

---

### **2. Refund Eligibility**

#### **2.1 When Refunds Are Available**

Refunds may be available in the following circumstances:

**A. Unauthorized Purchases**
• If a purchase was made without your authorization
• If your account was compromised or accessed by unauthorized persons
• You must report unauthorized purchases within 48 hours of the transaction

**B. Technical Issues**
• If Coins were not credited to your account after successful payment
• If you did not receive the purchased item or service
• Technical errors preventing purchase completion
• Duplicate charges for the same transaction

**C. Payment Errors**
• If you were charged multiple times for a single purchase
• If the transaction amount charged differs from the displayed amount
• Payment gateway errors resulting in failed transactions

**D. Legal Requirements**
• As required by applicable consumer protection laws and regulations
• Consumer protection laws in your jurisdiction

#### **2.2 Refund Timeframes**

• **Standard Refund Window:** 48 hours from purchase date
• **Unauthorized Purchase:** 7 days from transaction date
• **Technical Issues:** 14 days from purchase date
• **Duplicate Charges:** 30 days from transaction date

**Note:** Refund requests submitted after these timeframes may not be eligible for refund, except as required by applicable law.

---

### **3. Non-Refundable Items**

#### **3.1 Generally Non-Refundable**

The following purchases are generally **NOT** eligible for refund:

**A. Virtual Currency (Coins)**
• Coins that have been successfully credited to your account
• Coins that have been used, spent, or transferred
• Coins purchased more than 48 hours ago (unless unauthorized or technical error)
• Coins used for gifts, video calls, or other in-app services

**B. Digital Services**
• Premium features already accessed or used
• Gifts sent to other users
• Services already consumed (video calls, live streaming, etc.)
• Digital content already delivered

**C. User Error**
• Accidental purchases made by the user
• Change of mind after purchase
• Purchases made by mistake
• Failure to read purchase details before confirming

**D. Account Violations**
• Accounts suspended or terminated for policy violations
• Fraudulent activity or abuse of the refund system
• Violation of Terms & Conditions

#### **3.2 Exceptions**

Even if an item is generally non-refundable, we may provide refunds in the following cases:
• As required by applicable law
• In cases of technical errors on our part
• For unauthorized transactions
• At our sole discretion for exceptional circumstances

---

### **4. How to Request a Refund**

#### **4.1 Refund Request Process**

**Step 1: Contact Our Support Team**
• Email: **refunds@chamakz.app**
• Subject: "Refund Request - [Your Transaction ID]"
• Include all required information (see below)

**Step 2: Provide Required Information**
Your refund request must include:
• Transaction ID or Payment Receipt Number
• Purchase Date and Time
• Amount Charged
• Payment Method Used (UPI, Google Play, etc.)
• Reason for Refund Request
• Your Account Email/Phone Number
• Any supporting documents (screenshots, receipts, etc.)

**Step 3: Review Process**
• We will acknowledge your request within 24-48 hours
• We will review your request within 5-7 business days
• We may request additional information if needed
• You will be notified of the decision via email

**Step 4: Refund Processing**
• If approved, refund will be processed within 7-14 business days
• Refund method depends on your original payment method
• Processing time may vary by payment provider

#### **4.2 Refund Methods**

**UPI Payments:**
• Refunded to your original UPI account
• Processing time: 7-14 business days
• You will receive confirmation via email

**Google Play Store Purchases:**
• Processed through Google Play Store
• Follows Google Play Store's refund policy
• Standard window: 48 hours (may vary by region)
• After 48 hours, contact us at refunds@chamakz.app

**Other Payment Methods:**
• Refunded via original payment method
• Processing time varies by payment provider
• Contact us for specific timelines

#### **4.3 Refund Amount**

• **Full Refund:** For unauthorized purchases, technical errors, or duplicate charges
• **Partial Refund:** May apply if Coins were partially used (at our discretion)
• **Processing Fees:** May be deducted as per payment gateway policies
• **Currency:** Refunds will be processed in the same currency as the original purchase

### **5. Special Cases**

#### **5.1 Google Play Store Purchases**

For purchases made through Google Play Store:
• Refunds are subject to Google Play Store's refund policy
• You may request refunds directly through Google Play Store within 48 hours
• After 48 hours, contact us at refunds@chamakz.app
• We will review and process refunds according to this Policy

#### **5.2 UPI Payments (PayPrime)**

For purchases made via UPI through PayPrime:
• Refund requests must be submitted within 48 hours for best results
• Refunds processed to your original UPI account
• Processing time: 7-14 business days
• Contact: refunds@chamakz.app with transaction details

#### **5.3 Account Termination**

If your account is terminated:
• Unused Coins may be forfeited
• Refunds are generally not available for accounts terminated due to policy violations
• Exceptions may apply for technical issues or unauthorized access
• Contact us for specific cases

#### **5.4 App Discontinuation**

If Chamakz is discontinued or services are terminated:
• We will provide reasonable notice (minimum 30 days)
• Unused Coins may be refunded at our discretion
• Users will be notified via email and in-app notification
• Refund process will be communicated at that time

### **6. Dispute Resolution**

#### **6.1 Disputes**

If you disagree with our refund decision:
• Contact us at refunds@chamakz.app with additional information
• Provide any supporting documents or evidence
• We will review your case again within 5-7 business days
• We are committed to fair resolution of disputes

#### **6.2 Escalation**

If the issue remains unresolved after our review:
• You may contact your payment provider (Google Play, UPI bank, etc.)
• You may contact consumer protection authorities in your jurisdiction
• We are committed to resolving disputes fairly and in accordance with applicable law

#### **6.3 Limitations**

• Refund decisions are final after our review process
• We reserve the right to refuse refunds for policy violations or fraudulent activity
• Legal requirements take precedence over this Policy
• This Policy does not limit your rights under applicable consumer protection laws
