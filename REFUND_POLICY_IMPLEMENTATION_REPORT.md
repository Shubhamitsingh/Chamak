## 📝 Detailed Refund Policy Content

### **Section 1: Introduction & Scope**

**Content:**
```
This Refund Policy ("Policy") governs refund requests for purchases made 
through the Chamakz mobile application ("App"). By making a purchase, you 
agree to this Refund Policy. This Policy applies to:

• Virtual currency ("Coins") purchases
• In-app purchases and digital goods
• All payment methods (UPI, Google Play Billing, etc.)

This Policy is in addition to our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy. 
Please read this Policy carefully before making any purchase.
```

**Rationale:**
- Clear scope definition
- Covers all purchase types
- References other policies

---

### **Section 2: Refund Eligibility**

**Content:**
```
2.1 General Refund Eligibility

Refunds may be available in the following circumstances:

A. Unauthorized Purchases
   • If a purchase was made without your authorization
   • If your account was compromised
   • You must report within 48 hours of the transaction

B. Technical Issues
   • If Coins were not credited to your account after successful payment
   • If you did not receive the purchased item/service
   • Technical errors preventing purchase completion

C. Duplicate Charges
   • If you were charged multiple times for a single purchase
   • If the same transaction was processed more than once

D. Legal Requirements
   • As required by applicable laws and regulations
   • Consumer protection laws in your jurisdiction

2.2 Refund Timeframes

• Standard Refund Window: 48 hours from purchase
• Unauthorized Purchase: 7 days from transaction date
• Technical Issues: 14 days from purchase date
• Duplicate Charges: 30 days from transaction date

Note: Refund requests submitted after these timeframes may not be eligible 
for refund, except as required by law.
```

**Rationale:**
- Clear eligibility criteria
- Specific timeframes
- Covers common scenarios
- Protects both user and business

---

### **Section 3: Non-Refundable Items**

**Content:**
```
3.1 Generally Non-Refundable

The following purchases are generally NOT eligible for refund:

A. Virtual Currency (Coins)
   • Coins that have been credited to your account
   • Coins that have been used/spent
   • Coins purchased more than 48 hours ago (unless unauthorized)

B. Digital Services
   • Premium features already accessed
   • Gifts sent to other users
   • Services already consumed (video calls, live streaming)

C. User Error
   • Accidental purchases
   • Change of mind
   • Purchases made by mistake

D. Account Violations
   • Accounts suspended or terminated for policy violations
   • Fraudulent activity or abuse

3.2 Exceptions

Even if an item is generally non-refundable, we may provide refunds:
• As required by law
• In cases of technical errors
• For unauthorized transactions
• At our sole discretion for exceptional circumstances
```

**Rationale:**
- Protects business from abuse
- Clear exceptions
- Prevents refund fraud
- Maintains flexibility

---

### **Section 4: Refund Process**

**Content:**
```
4.1 How to Request a Refund

Step 1: Contact Support
   • Email: refunds@chamakz.app
   • Include: Transaction ID, Purchase Date, Amount, Reason

Step 2: Provide Information
   • Payment receipt/screenshot
   • Transaction details
   • Account information
   • Reason for refund request

Step 3: Review Process
   • We will review your request within 5-7 business days
   • We may request additional information
   • You will be notified of the decision via email

Step 4: Refund Processing
   • If approved, refund will be processed within 7-14 business days
   • Refund method depends on original payment method
   • Processing time may vary by payment provider

4.2 Refund Methods

• UPI Payments: Refunded to original UPI account (7-14 business days)
• Google Play: Processed through Google Play Store (follows Google's policy)
• Other Methods: Refunded via original payment method

4.3 Refund Amount

• Full Refund: For unauthorized purchases or technical errors
• Partial Refund: May apply if Coins were partially used
• Processing Fees: May be deducted as per payment gateway policies
```

**Rationale:**
- Clear step-by-step process
- Realistic timeframes
- Multiple contact methods
- Transparent refund methods

---

### **Section 5: Special Cases**

**Content:**
```
5.1 Google Play Store Purchases

For purchases made through Google Play Store:
• Refunds are subject to Google Play Store's refund policy
• You may request refunds directly through Google Play Store
• Standard refund window: 48 hours (may vary by region)
• After 48 hours, contact us at refunds@chamakz.app

5.2 UPI Payments (PayPrime)

For purchases made via UPI:
• Refund requests must be submitted within 48 hours
• Refunds processed to original UPI account
• Processing time: 7-14 business days
• Contact: refunds@chamakz.app with transaction details

5.3 Account Termination

If your account is terminated:
• Unused Coins may be forfeited
• Refunds are not available for policy violations
• Exceptions may apply for technical issues

5.4 App Discontinuation

If Chamakz is discontinued:
• We will provide reasonable notice
• Unused Coins may be refunded (at our discretion)
• Users will be notified via email and in-app notification
```

**Rationale:**
- Covers different payment methods
- Addresses edge cases
- Protects business interests
- User-friendly exceptions

---

### **Section 6: Dispute Resolution**

**Content:**
```
6.1 Disputes

If you disagree with our refund decision:
• Contact us at refunds@chamakz.app with additional information
• We will review your case again
• Provide any supporting documents

6.2 Escalation

If the issue remains unresolved:
• You may contact your payment provider (Google Play, UPI bank)
• Consumer protection authorities in your jurisdiction
• We are committed to resolving disputes fairly

6.3 Limitations

• Refund decisions are final after review
• We reserve the right to refuse refunds for policy violations
• Legal requirements take precedence over this Policy
```

**Rationale:**
- Fair dispute process
- Clear escalation path
- Protects business rights
- Legal compliance
