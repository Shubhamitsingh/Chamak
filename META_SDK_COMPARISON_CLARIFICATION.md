## 📊 **Two Different SDKs - Which One Do You Need?**

### **SDK #1: Meta App Events SDK** ⭐ **YOU NEED THIS**

**Purpose:** Track app installs and events FOR Meta ad campaigns

**What It Does:**
- ✅ Tracks when someone installs your app
- ✅ Tracks when someone opens your app
- ✅ Tracks purchases in your app
- ✅ Sends data to Meta for campaign optimization
- ✅ Measures which ads led to installs

**When You Need It:**
- ✅ Running app install campaigns on Facebook/Instagram
- ✅ Want to measure campaign effectiveness
- ✅ Want Meta to optimize your campaigns
- ✅ Want to track installs from ads

**Cost:** FREE

**Is It Compulsory?**
- Technically: No
- Practically: YES - Without it, campaigns won't work well

---

### **SDK #2: Meta Audience Network SDK** ❌ **YOU DON'T NEED THIS**

**Purpose:** Show ads INSIDE your app to earn money

**What It Does:**
- Shows banner ads in your app
- Shows full-screen ads
- Shows video ads
- You earn money when users see/click ads

**When You Need It:**
- You want to monetize your app with ads
- You want to earn revenue from showing ads
- You want to show ads between screens

**Cost:** FREE (you earn money, not pay)

**Is It Compulsory?**
- No - Only if you want to show ads in your app
